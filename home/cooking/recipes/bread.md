# Bread

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** no — carb side for France and the kids.
- **Status:** Guillaume's version (bread machine, 2026-10-30). *(Unknown: which flour and
  recipe, which program, loaf size.)*
- **Serves:** one loaf — more than France and the kids eat with one dinner; the rest does
  for breakfast.

## Ingredients

Typical for a ~750 g machine loaf — not yet confirmed, check against the machine's manual:

- 500 g flour (Swiss "Halbweissmehl" or "Ruchmehl", or plain white flour)
- 10 g salt
- 7 g dried yeast
- ~330 ml lukewarm water

## Steps

1. Into the machine's pan in the order its manual says — usually water first, then flour,
   salt in one corner, yeast on top, kept apart from the salt and the water.
2. Pick the program and loaf size, start.
3. Take the loaf out as soon as it's done. Cool on a rack at least 1 h before cutting.

## Notes

- Hands-off, and it leaves the oven free. The delay timer can have it ready at a set time;
  with dried yeast and no milk or eggs in the dough, it's safe to leave overnight.
