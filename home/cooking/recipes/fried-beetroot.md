# Fried beetroot

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes. Beetroot is one of the sweeter vegetables (~7–8 g
  net carbs per 100 g), but his diet is about starches, so it's fine.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version. Known: homemade, i.e. from raw
  beetroot.
- **Serves:** the whole family, ~1 medium beetroot (~150 g) per adult and for Oscar, about
  half that for Augustin — ~4 medium beetroots total.

## Ingredients

- Raw beetroot (pre-cooked vacuum-packed "Randen" is a quicker shortcut)
- Butter or olive oil
- Salt, pepper, fresh or dried thyme
- Optional: a few drops of balsamic vinegar at the end

## Steps

**Raw beetroot:**

1. Peel (gloves help), slice thinly ~3–5 mm.
2. Fry over medium heat with a lid on for ~10 min to soften, then lid off and turn up the
   heat for a few more minutes to brown. Thicker pieces take longer — or simmer them first.
3. Season with salt, pepper, thyme at the end.

**Pre-cooked beetroot** (shortcut, not the usual way):

1. Cut into wedges or ~1 cm slices, pat dry.
2. Medium-high heat, butter or oil, 5–8 min turning occasionally, until the edges caramelise.
3. Season with salt, pepper, thyme at the end.

## Notes

- Beetroot stains everything — cutting board, hands, and anything it touches on the plate
  (including the halloumi and spätzli). Some kids find pink spätzli fun, some don't.
