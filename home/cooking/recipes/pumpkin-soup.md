# Pumpkin soup

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — shared main. Pumpkin is a sweeter vegetable, not a
  starch (see [roasted pumpkin](roasted-pumpkin.md)), and coconut milk is low-carb. Needs a
  protein next to it on his plate: sausages, or crispy bacon on top.
- **Status:** Guillaume's version (ingredients, 2026-10-30): coconut milk, onions, garlic.
  *(Unknown: roasted or raw pumpkin, stock or water, any spices, quantities — the ones
  below are estimates.)*
- **Serves:** the whole family as a main (with sausages and bread), ~1.5 L: ~1 kg pumpkin
  flesh, i.e. one ~1.2 kg Hokkaido.

## Ingredients

- ~1 kg pumpkin flesh — roasted, or raw in ~3 cm cubes
- 2 onions, chopped
- 2–3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
- ~600 ml stock or water
- Oil, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Soften the onions in a little oil, ~5 min. Add the garlic for the last minute.
2. Add the pumpkin and the stock or water. Simmer ~10 min if the pumpkin is already
   roasted, ~20 min if raw, until it falls apart.
3. Add the coconut milk, blend until smooth, season. Thin with water if it's too thick.

## Notes

- With pumpkin already roasted (extra from a [roasted pumpkin](roasted-pumpkin.md) side),
  it's a ~20 min job.
- Make-ahead friendly: it keeps 2–3 days in the fridge and reheats in 5 min.
- To eat outside, a thermos keeps it hot for hours — fill it with boiling water first to
  preheat it.
