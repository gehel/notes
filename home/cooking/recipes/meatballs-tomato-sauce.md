# Meatballs in tomato sauce

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — meatballs and sauce, with a big share of the
  roasted vegetables instead of the pasta. The little breadcrumb in the meatballs doesn't
  count; his diet isn't that strict.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** ~600 g meat, ~24 meatballs: Guillaume and both kids, plus a portion set aside
  for France. Pasta for the kids (and France): ~120 g dry per adult and for Oscar, ~80–100 g
  for Augustin. Vegetables: ~1.5 kg in all — in the sauce and on a roasting tray, so
  Guillaume has plenty without the pasta.

## Ingredients

**Meatballs:**

- 600 g minced meat — beef, or half beef, half pork
- 1 egg
- 1 slice of stale bread soaked in a little milk and squeezed out, or ~40 g breadcrumbs
- ~30 g Parmesan, grated
- 1 small onion, grated or very finely chopped; 1 garlic clove, crushed
- Parsley, 1 tsp salt, pepper

**Sauce:**

- 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves
- 2 carrots, grated, and 1 courgette, finely diced — they melt into the sauce
- 2 cans (400 g) chopped tomatoes, or ~700 g passata
- 1 tsp dried oregano or a few basil leaves
- Olive oil, salt, a pinch of sugar

**Roasted vegetables:**

- 1 cauliflower, in florets
- ~500 g carrots, in sticks
- Olive oil, salt; optional: a pinch of cumin or paprika

## Steps

1. Oven to 200 °C. Toss the cauliflower and carrots with oil and salt on a lined tray,
   in one layer. Roast ~30 min, turning once, until browned at the edges.
2. Mix the meatball ingredients by hand, just until they hold together — overworked, they
   go tough. Roll ~30 g balls with wet hands.
3. Brown them in a large pan with a little oil, in batches, turning, ~5 min. They don't
   need to cook through. Set aside.
4. Same pan: soften the onion, grated carrot and courgette ~8 min, add the garlic for 1
   min, then the tomatoes and herbs. Simmer ~10 min.
5. Put the meatballs back, lid half on, simmer ~15 min until cooked through. Season.
6. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in well-salted water.

## Notes

- ~50 min, ~20 of it hands-on. Rolling the meatballs is a job the kids can do.
- Less splatter, less standing at the stove: bake the meatballs on a lined tray, 200 °C
  ~15 min, on a second tray next to the vegetables, instead of step 3.
- Stale homemade [bread](bread.md) is the better binder — it keeps the meatballs tender.
- Reheats well: a portion keeps in the pan or a container for whoever eats later. Extra
  meatballs freeze, raw or cooked.
