# Spätzli

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** no — carb side for the girlfriend and kids.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version. Known: always homemade.
  *(Unknown: press/hobel/board, any additions like cheese or herbs.)*
- **Serves:** girlfriend + both kids (7 and 10) as a side. Scale ~100 g flour and 1 egg per
  person; 300 g leaves a little margin with kids' portions.

## Ingredients

- 300 g flour (Swiss "Knöpfli-/Spätzlimehl" or plain white flour)
- 3 eggs
- ~125 ml water or milk (or half-half)
- 1 tsp salt, a pinch of nutmeg
- Butter, for tossing at the end

## Steps

1. Beat everything into a thick, sticky batter until it's smooth and starts to form bubbles
   (a wooden spoon, ~2–3 min). It should drip slowly off the spoon, not run.
2. Rest 20–30 min.
3. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a gentle simmer (not a rolling boil).
4. Press the batter into the water in batches. They're done when they float, ~1 min after.
5. Lift out with a slotted spoon, drain, toss in a hot pan with butter. Optionally let them
   take a bit of colour.

## Notes

- Cooked spätzli keep warm in the buttered pan, so they can be done first and the halloumi
  last.
