# Butter chicken

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes, without the rice — chicken in a tomato, butter and
  cream sauce is low-carb. In the evening, the green beans replace the rice on his plate.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** the whole family as a main with [rice](rice.md): ~700 g chicken, ~600 g green
  beans.

## Ingredients

- ~700 g boneless chicken thighs (or breast), in bite-size pieces
- 40 g butter, a little oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves and a thumb of ginger, grated
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp mild paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp turmeric
- 400 g passata (or a can of chopped tomatoes, blended)
- ~150 ml cream
- Salt, a pinch of sugar if the tomatoes are sharp
- Optional: fresh coriander; chili flakes or a fresh chili

**Green beans** (the vegetable side):

- ~600 g green beans, topped
- 1 garlic clove, sliced; butter or oil; salt

## Steps

1. Season the chicken with salt and 1 tsp of the garam masala. Brown it in a hot pan with a
   little oil, in batches, ~3–4 min. It doesn't need to cook through. Set aside.
2. Same pan, medium heat, butter: soften the onion ~5–7 min, until golden. Add the garlic,
   ginger and the rest of the spices for 1 min.
3. Add the passata, simmer ~10 min. Blend it smooth if you like.
4. Put the chicken back with the cream. Simmer ~8–10 min, until the chicken is cooked
   through. Season.
5. Meanwhile, boil the green beans in salted water ~6–8 min, until just tender. Drain, toss
   in a pan with butter or oil and the garlic for 1–2 min.

## Notes

- ~35 min, most of it simmering. Start the [rice](rice.md) first — it takes ~45 min.
- Heat to taste: add the chili with the spices, or serve it on the side.
- **Reheats very well**, even better the next day. For someone eating later: sauce in one
  container, rice in another, both reheated until piping hot.
- Leftover [roast chicken](roast-chicken.md) works: skip step 1 and add it at step 4, just
  long enough to heat through (~5 min).
- More time ahead: marinate the raw chicken in ~150 g plain yogurt with the salt and spices
  the night before, for more tender meat.
