# Roast chicken with vegetables

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — shared main, chicken plus the vegetables from the
  tray. He takes more of the vegetables instead of the potatoes.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** the whole family. A ~1.8 kg chicken for 4, with Guillaume's share bigger
  since he has no carb side. A ~2 kg bird leaves some for the next day's lunch.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, ~1.8–2 kg
- Butter or olive oil
- 1 lemon, 1 head of garlic (halved across), a few sprigs of thyme
- Vegetables for the tray, cut in big chunks: ~1.3 kg carrots and 1 celeriac. 1 kg carrots
  and 1 celeriac was only just enough for 4 (2026-11-01). Fennel and onions also work.
- Salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 min ahead. Oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan).
2. Pat the skin dry. Rub with butter or oil, salt well inside and out. Put the lemon
   (halved), garlic and thyme in the cavity.
3. Toss the vegetables with oil and salt in a roasting tray, put the chicken on top.
4. Roast ~1h15–1h30 (~20 min per 500 g + 20 min). It's done when the juice from the thigh
   runs clear, or 75 °C in the thickest part of the thigh.
5. Rest the chicken 10–15 min before carving. The vegetables can stay in the oven meanwhile.

## Notes

- Dry skin is crisp skin — pat it dry, and don't cover it.
- The vegetables under the chicken get the drippings; stir them once halfway.
- Serve with [roasted potatoes](roasted-potatoes.md) for the others — they share the oven.
