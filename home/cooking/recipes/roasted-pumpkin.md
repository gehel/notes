# Roasted pumpkin

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes. Pumpkin is one of the sweeter vegetables (~6–10 g
  net carbs per 100 g depending on the variety, butternut at the top end), like beetroot —
  his diet is about starches, so it's fine.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version. Known: oven-roasted.
  *(Unknown: which pumpkin, how it's cut, seasoning.)*
- **Serves:** the whole family as a side, ~1–1.5 kg whole pumpkin (one Hokkaido).

## Ingredients

- Pumpkin — Hokkaido (no need to peel, the skin softens) or butternut (peel it)
- Olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- Optional: thyme or rosemary, chili flakes, a pinch of cumin

## Steps

1. Oven to 200 °C. Halve, scoop out the seeds, cut into ~3 cm cubes or wedges.
2. Toss with oil, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a lined tray — crowded pieces
   steam instead of browning.
3. Roast ~25–35 min, turning once, until tender with browned edges.

## Notes

- Roasting extra costs nothing: leftovers keep 3–4 days in the fridge, and turn into a
  ~20 min [soup](pumpkin-soup.md).
