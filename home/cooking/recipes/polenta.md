# Polenta

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** no — cornmeal is a starch. Carb side in the evening,
  or a lunch main for everyone.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version. *(Unknown: which polenta,
  stovetop or oven.)*
- **Serves:** the whole family as a lunch main: 300 g coarse polenta (~80 g per adult,
  ~60 g per kid, a little margin).

## Ingredients

- 300 g coarse polenta (Ticino "Bramata")
- ~1.3 L liquid — half milk, half water or stock
- 1 tsp salt
- ~40 g butter, ~50 g Parmesan, grated

## Steps

**Oven** (hands-off, when the oven is free):

1. Oven to 180 °C. Bring the liquid and salt to a boil in an oven-proof pot.
2. Whisk in the polenta in a slow stream, stirring until it thickens, ~2 min.
3. Lid on, into the oven ~40–45 min. Stir once halfway.
4. Stir in the butter and Parmesan. Add a splash of hot liquid if it's too stiff.

**Stovetop:** same, but simmer on the lowest heat ~40–50 min, lid half on, stirring often
— it spits.

## Notes

- Bramata needs its ~45 min; instant/fine polenta is ~5 min but less flavour.
- Leftovers set firm: slice and pan-fry them the next day.
