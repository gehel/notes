# Mushroom ragout with leek and spinach

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — mushrooms, leek and spinach are all low-carb. As an
  evening main it needs a protein next to it (steak, chicken, fried eggs).
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** the whole family over [polenta](polenta.md): ~600 g mushrooms.

## Ingredients

- ~600 g mushrooms — button and brown mushrooms, plus wild ones if any are in season
- Optional: ~10 g dried porcini, soaked 15 min in hot water (keep the water)
- 2 leeks, sliced into half-rings and washed
- 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- ~200 g fresh spinach (or chard)
- Olive oil, butter
- A splash of white wine or stock; optional ~100 ml cream
- Thyme, parsley, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Fry the mushrooms in hot oil, in batches, without moving them for the first few
   minutes, until browned. Set aside.
2. Same pan, medium heat, butter: soften the onion and leek ~8 min. Add the garlic and
   thyme for the last minute.
3. Put the mushrooms back (and the porcini). Add the wine or stock (and the porcini water),
   simmer 2–3 min. Stir in the cream if using.
4. Wilt in the spinach, ~1 min. Season, finish with parsley.

## Notes

- Crowded mushrooms release their water and boil instead of browning — hence the batches.
  Salt them at the end, not at the start.
- ~25 min. With oven polenta, start the ragout ~20 min after the polenta goes in.
- A fried egg on top adds protein for anyone who wants more.
