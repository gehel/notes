# Rice

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** no — carb side for France and the kids.
- **Status:** Guillaume's version (rice cooker, 2026-10-29). *(Unknown: which rice.)*
- **Serves:** France + both kids (7 and 10) as a side: **2 measures**, the rice cooker's
  minimum. That's more than the three eat, so there are always leftovers.

## Ingredients

- Rice, measured with the rice cooker's graduated cup
- Water, to the matching line in the cooker bowl

## Steps

1. Measure the rice with the cup. Rinse until the water runs mostly clear.
2. Into the cooker bowl, water up to the line for that number of cups.
3. Start the cooker. Let it rest on "keep warm" ~10 min after it clicks, then fluff.

## Notes

- Hands-off: start it ~45 min before serving and do the rest of the meal around it.
- **Leftovers are built in.** Cool them quickly and refrigerate within an hour or two; eat
  within a day, reheated until hot. Good for fried rice at lunch — there's no carb
  restriction then.
