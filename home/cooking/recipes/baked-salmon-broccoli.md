# Oven-baked salmon with broccoli

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — shared main, and a light one. The others add a carb
  side ([rice](rice.md) or boiled potatoes); Guillaume takes more broccoli instead.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** the whole family: ~650 g salmon (~180 g for Guillaume since he has no carb
  side, ~150 g each for France and Oscar, ~100–120 g for Augustin) and 2 heads of broccoli
  (~800 g).

## Ingredients

- ~650 g salmon fillet, skin on (one piece or 4 portions)
- 2 heads of broccoli
- Olive oil, butter
- 1 lemon
- Salt, pepper; dill or parsley
- Optional: flaked almonds, toasted in a dry pan, on the broccoli

## Steps

1. Oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan). Salmon skin down on a lined tray, pat dry. Oil, salt,
   pepper, a few lemon slices on top.
2. Bake ~12–15 min, depending on thickness. It's done when it just flakes and the centre
   has turned opaque.
3. Meanwhile, cut the broccoli into florets; peel and slice the stalk too. Steam ~4–5 min,
   or boil in salted water ~3–4 min, until just tender and still bright green.
4. Toss the broccoli with a little butter or olive oil, salt and lemon juice. Herbs and the
   rest of the lemon on the salmon.

## Notes

- ~25 min, and the salmon needs no attention in the oven.
- Check for pin bones before cooking — run a finger along the fillet and pull them out with
  tweezers.
- Eating later (e.g. after a training): the salmon is fine lukewarm or cold, the broccoli
  reheats in a minute.
- One-tray variant: broccoli florets tossed in oil and salt go in the oven first; the
  salmon joins them 5 min later. Roasted broccoli gets browned edges instead of staying
  green.
- A lean white fish (cod, perch) is lighter still: same method, ~10 min.
