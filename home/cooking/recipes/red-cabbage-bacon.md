# Red cabbage with bacon

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — shared main. Red cabbage is low in carbs (~4–5 g net
  per 100 g).
- **Status:** Guillaume's version (method, 2026-10-29). Quick-sautéed, not braised.
  *(Bacon quantity is an estimate, not yet confirmed.)*
- **Serves:** the whole family. 1 large red cabbage and ~300 g bacon for 4, with bacon as the
  protein — Guillaume's share is bigger since he has no carb side. 1 large cabbage is enough
  for 4 (confirmed 2026-10-30).

## Ingredients

- 1 large red cabbage, quartered, cored, sliced
- ~300 g smoked bacon (lardons or strips)
- Oil or butter for the cabbage (not needed with the bacon-first variant)
- Salt, pepper
- Vinegar

## Steps

1. Sauté the cabbage in a pan until it's just softened, then take it out and set aside.
2. Fry the bacon in the same pan.
3. Put the cabbage back with the bacon.
4. Season with salt, pepper and vinegar.

**Bacon-first variant** (trying on 2026-10-30): fry the bacon first and set it aside, sauté
the cabbage in the bacon fat, then put the bacon back. Saves the oil, adds flavour to the
cabbage, and keeps the bacon crisp. A large cabbage in batches may soak up all the fat — top
up with a little oil if the pan goes dry.

## Notes

- A large cabbage is a lot for one pan — sauté it in batches if it doesn't fit in one layer,
  otherwise it steams instead of frying.
- Quick to make (~20 min), best served fresh.
