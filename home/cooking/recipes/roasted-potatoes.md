# Roasted potatoes

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** no — carb side for France and the kids.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** France + both kids (7 and 10) as a side: ~700 g potatoes.

## Ingredients

- ~700 g floury or all-purpose potatoes ("mehligkochend")
- Olive oil (or fat from the roast)
- Salt, rosemary or thyme

## Steps

1. Peel, cut into ~4 cm chunks. Boil in salted water ~8 min, until just tender at the
   edges.
2. Drain, let them steam dry 2 min, then shake them in the pot to rough up the edges —
   rough edges make the crust.
3. Toss with oil, salt and herbs. Spread in one layer on a tray of their own (not under the
   chicken, or they steam).
4. Roast ~45 min at 200 °C, turning once, until golden and crisp.

## Notes

- With [roast chicken](roast-chicken.md): put them in ~45 min before the chicken is done.
  While the chicken rests, turn the oven up to 220 °C so they finish crisp.
