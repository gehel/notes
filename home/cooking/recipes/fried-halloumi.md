# Fried halloumi

- **Evening plate for Guillaume:** yes — it's his main protein on carb-free evenings.
- **Status:** generic baseline, not yet Guillaume's version.
- **Serves:** the whole family, ~450 g total (two 250 g blocks): ~150 g for Guillaume
  since he has no carb side, ~100 g for France, ~120 g for Oscar (a full portion and then
  some), ~80 g for Augustin (both seen 2026-10-29).

## Ingredients

- Halloumi, sliced ~1 cm thick
- A little olive oil (optional — halloumi releases its own fat)
- To finish: lemon juice, black pepper, dried oregano or chili flakes

## Steps

1. Pat the slices dry with kitchen paper — dry cheese browns, wet cheese steams.
2. Non-stick pan, medium-high heat, very little or no oil.
3. 2–3 min per side, until deep golden. Don't move them before then, or they stick.
4. Finish with lemon and pepper, serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Augustin (7) doesn't love halloumi**, but it goes down OK pan-fried — keep it to this
  method rather than grilled/raw-in-salad when cooking for the whole family. Confirmed
  2026-10-29: not a fan, but ate enough of it.
- **Fry it last.** Halloumi turns rubbery as it cools, so it's the one thing that can't wait
  on the table.
- Honey drizzle is a classic finish but doesn't fit Guillaume's plate — do it on the others'
  portions only if wanted.
